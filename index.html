About me

Who am I

I am an urban planner and designer with an education at two of Europe's top architecture universities, the Politecnico of Turin and the Politecnico of Barcelona. I design both public and interior spaces.

I design environments where every line, colour and reflection speaks of a deeper connection with place and people. My work grows from a love for art, aesthetics and design, and from a curiosity for neuroaesthetics: how spaces can transform lives.

I am drawn to materials that tell stories, to the dialogue between function and beauty, and to spaces that feel alive, intentional and meaningful. Each project is an exploration of detail and experience.

How I work

Principles

01

Genius loci

Every project starts from the place: its history, climate, light and the people who use it. Nothing is added that the site does not ask for.

02

Function and beauty

A constant dialogue between the two. A space has to work every day, and it has to feel alive, intentional and meaningful.

03

Sustainability & resilience

Honest, durable and sustainable materials, climate-adaptive choices and nature-based solutions, so that places age well and can face change.

01

Civic library project proposal

Venaria Reale

Adaptive reuse · Library · Public space

Where a barracks once closed ranks, the doors now open. The conversion of an existing ex-military barracks into a public library, with the public space around it as its reading room: a timber-lined portico, a quiet arcade and a street that invites you in.

02

Medieval church ruins adaptive use proposal

Emilia Romagna

Heritage · Restoration and green hub

Antique ruins, kept as found and given a second life as a restoration and green hub. Brick vaults and lime-white walls frame linen drapes, timber platforms and olive trees: a new beginning for an abandoned place.

03

Urban garden regeneration proposal

Venaria Reale

Abandoned buildings · Cultural green hub

Abandoned buildings become a cultural green hub. Brick arcades, courtyards and old halls are stitched together by planted beds, walnut benches, terracotta paths and light linen sails, turning leftover space into a garden the whole town can use.
